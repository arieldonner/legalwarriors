Conservatorships

Guidance for families supporting loved ones with special needs.

Conservatorships provide a legal way for families to help protect and care for adults who can’t manage their own finances or health decisions. Our firm helps you navigate the process with compassion and clarity.

Frequently Asked Questions

A conservatorship is a legal arrangement where a court appoints someone to manage personal or financial affairs for an adult who cannot do so themselves.

It depends on the complexity of the case and the court schedule, but most conservatorships take a few months from filing to final hearing.

While you can technically file on your own, conservatorship cases are complex. Having an experienced attorney ensures the process goes smoothly and avoids costly mistakes.
